03 / Behind the rolls

How this works

Every floor counts. Each floor gives one normal-table roll, plus a separate chance at an elite clue scroll and eligible unique rewards.

Depth matters. Common stacks use the Wiki's level-3 quantities and per-depth truncating multiplier; guaranteed demon tears begin at level 3.

Uniques unlock. Cloth from 2, Eye of ayak from 3, treads from 4, and Dom from 6. Equipment rates improve through level 9 and then cap.

Claimed runs only. Failed descents that forfeit rewards, existing elite clue possession and extra charges added to an equipped Eye are not simulated.

See drop-rate assumptions

The common table has 312 weighted tickets (e.g., 7/104 = 21/312). The equipment roll is mutually exclusive within a floor; the pet and clue scroll have independent rolls. Gear odds are 1/2,500 per eligible item at delve 2, 1/2,000 at 3, 1/1,350 at 4, 1/810 at 5, 1/765 at 6, 1/720 at 7, 1/630 at 8, and 1/540 at 9+. Dom is 1/1,000 at 6, 1/750 at 7, 1/500 at 8 and 1/250 at 9+. Elite clues are 1/75 at floors 1–2 and 1/50 at floors 3+.
